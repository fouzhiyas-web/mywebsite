About Us

A legacy of learning, a vision for tomorrow.

📜

Our History

Founded in 1998 with just 120 students and two departments, Sunrise Institute has grown into a premier multi-disciplinary campus. Over two decades we have built strong industry partnerships, research centres and a vibrant alumni network spread across 40 countries.

🎯

Our Vision

To be a globally respected centre of academic excellence that nurtures innovative thinkers, ethical leaders and problem solvers who create a positive impact on society and technology.

🚀

Our Mission

To deliver outcome-based education, promote research and entrepreneurship, and provide an inclusive environment where every learner can discover and achieve their potential.
